Les réalisations pérennes




New Preserved Buildings

The Olympic Aquatic Center

Located in Saint-Denis, it hosted artistic swimming and water polo events during the Games. It will be open to the public and serve the residents as a leisure space, a place for learning to swim, and a training facility for local clubs. It will become an important place for sports and health in a rapidly developing region.

The Athletes' Village

The Athletes' Village, which hosted nearly 14,000 athletes during the Games, will be converted into a new sustainable residential neighborhood. This neighborhood will offer social and private housing, offices, and public infrastructure such as schools and shops for the residents of Seine-Saint-Denis.

The Media Village

The Media Village, located in Dugny in Seine-Saint-Denis, which served journalists during the Games, will be transformed into family housing and spaces adapted to the needs of the region's residents.


Temporary Buildings Dismantled

The Concorde Arena

Installed on the Place de la Concorde, which hosted urban sports competitions such as BMX freestyle, skateboarding, and breaking, will be dismantled to allow the Place de la Concorde to return to its original appearance.

The developments around the Trocadéro and the Eiffel Tower

The developments around the Trocadéro and the Eiffel Tower, which served for beach volleyball and other sports events, will be dismantled, and the iconic sites will return to their usual configuration.


Ancient Buildings

Le Stade de France

Locaated in Saint-Denis, it was the main venue for athletics events and the opening and closing ceremonies of the Games. It will continue to host major sports competitions, concerts, and various international events.

Le Grand Palais

The Grand Palais, which shosted the fencing and taekwondo events during the Olympics. It will return to its traditional role as a venue for cultural exhibitions and prestigious events.

L'Accor Arena

The Accor Arena (Bercy Arena), which was used for disciplines such as basketball and artistic gymnastics. It will continue to be a major venue for sports competitions, concerts, and other major events.

Le stade Roland-Garros

The Roland-Garros stadium, an iconic tennis site, which hosted not only tennis events but also boxing events. After the Games, it will continue to host the Roland-Garros tournament, one of the four Grand Slam tournaments.
